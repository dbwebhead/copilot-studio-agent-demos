# Brand Style Guide — Giving Tuesday (Demo)
> Tagline: **Clarity to impact—faster.**

## Voice & Tone
- Helpful, concise, confidence without hype.
- Donor‑centric (“you”-focused), plain US English, Grade 7–8 reading level.
- Avoid jargon. Use active verbs. Prefer short sentences.

## Messaging Pillars
1. **Impact first** — show outcomes people enable.
2. **Urgency, not pressure** — one day to act.
3. **Trust** — transparency and stewardship.

## Writing Rules
- Spell out numbers one–nine; use numerals for 10+.
- Oxford comma; AP style dates (e.g., Dec. 2, 2025).
- Avoid exclamation points; never shout in ALL CAPS.
- Inclusive language; people‑first phrasing.

## Accessibility
- Alt text for all imagery (≤125 chars), no “image of …” prefixes.
- Ensure color contrast **AA or better**.
- Links: meaningful, not “click here.”
- Buttons must be keyboard-focusable with visible focus rings.

## Email
- **Subject lines:** ≤55 characters; lead with benefit; 1 emoji max (optional).
- **Preheaders:** finish the thought; ≤85 characters.
- **CTAs:** single clear verb. Examples: *Give now*, *Share the link*, *Pledge monthly*.
- **Footers:** include EIN/tax language and contact options.

## Social
- Caption length ≤120 chars for primary posts.
- Always include a square image or short video (≤15s) with subtitles.
- Hashtags: `#GivingTuesday #GiveToDoGood` (2–3 total).

## Web
- Hero headline ≤7 words. Subhead ≤16 words.
- Primary button label: **Give now**. Secondary: **Learn more**.
- Progress bar updates at logical milestones (25/50/75/100%).

## Brand Elements
- **Primary color:** Accessible Blue `#166EB4` (text on white passes AA).
- **Neutrals:** Dark Gray `#333333`, Mid Gray `#666666`, Light Gray `#F5F6F8`.
- **Accent:** Emerald `#2BAE66` for success states.
- **Typefaces:** Segoe UI / Calibri (system fonts). Headings: semi‑bold; body: regular.
- **Buttons:** 8px corner radius; generous padding; hover darkens by 8–10%.

## Examples
**Subject line options**
- “Today only: your gift goes twice as far”
- “One day. One goal. Help families now.”
- “Make an impact before midnight”

**CTA options**
- Give now · Share the link · Start a monthly gift

**Alt text examples**
- “Volunteer hands a blanket to an elderly woman in a shelter”
- “Nurse checks a child’s pulse at a mobile clinic”

## Legal/Compliance
- Do not reference individual beneficiaries by name without consent.
- Use approved match language only after partner confirmation.
- No collection or display of phone numbers or sensitive personal data in examples.
