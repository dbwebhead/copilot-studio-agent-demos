# Giving Tuesday 2025 — Campaign Brief (Demo)

**Purpose:** Provide a concise plan for Giving Tuesday 2025 that can be executed inside Microsoft 365.

## 1) Overview
Giving Tuesday is a global day of generosity on the Tuesday after Thanksgiving. This campaign focuses on mobilizing current donors and engaged prospects to make a gift and share our mission.

## 2) Objective
- **Primary:** Raise $550,000 in 24 hours and acquire 2,500 new donors.  
- **Secondary:** Drive social shares and peer-to-peer referrals to expand reach.

## 3) Audience
- Active donors (last 24 months), lapsed 24–48 months, email subscribers, social followers.  
- Lookalike audiences built from recent donor and website engagement (no PII exported).

## 4) Message Pillars
- **Impact-first:** “Your gift today powers relief where it’s needed most.”  
- **Urgency:** “One day. One goal. Thousands helped.”  
- **Trust & transparency:** clear use-of-funds and outcomes.

## 5) Offer & CTA
- Core offer: Unrestricted gift matched 1:1 up to $150,000 by a corporate partner (pending).  
- **Primary CTA:** Give now · **Secondary CTA:** Share the campaign link with 3 friends.

## 6) Channels & Tactics
- **Email cadence:** teaser (T-3), launch (AM), reminder (PM), final push (evening).  
- **Social:** 4 organic posts (Mon night teaser; Tue morning launch; Tue noon impact; Tue 8pm final push).  
- **Paid:** social + search retargeting; tighten geography and intent keywords on the day.  
- **Web:** homepage hero + simple landing page with progress bar; mobile-first donate flow.

## 7) Content & Assets
- Hero image, short video (≤15s), 4 social tiles, 3 email banners, progress bar module.  
- Copy variants: 3 subject lines; short/medium/long bodies; captions ≤120 chars.  
- Accessibility: alt text, AA+ color contrast, keyboard focus on donate buttons.

## 8) Timeline
| Phase      | Dates (2025)     | Deliverables |
|------------|-------------------|--------------|
| Prep       | Nov 10–24         | Assets final; QA email + landing; schedule paid; publish teaser |
| Launch Day | Dec 2 (Giving Tue)| Emails ×3–4; 4 social posts; progress updates; donor thank-you |
| Follow-up  | Dec 3–10          | Receipt + impact; final totals; new donor welcome series |

## 9) Budget (rough)
- Paid media: $50,000 (social + search)  
- Creative & production: $8,000  
- Marketing ops: in-house

## 10) KPIs & Targets
- Revenue: $550,000; New donors: 2,500; Avg gift: $85  
- Email CTR ≥ 6%; Landing→Donation conversion ≥ 6%  
- ROAS ≥ 4.0 across paid media

## 11) Risks & Mitigations
- Match not confirmed → Plan B messaging; reduce target −15%  
- Site latency → Pre-warm cache; simplify page; add status link  
- Inbox competition → Subject line testing; stagger send times; strong preheaders

## 12) Governance & Compliance
- No export or sharing of PII outside tenant systems.  
- Follow brand style + WCAG 2.1 AA.  
- Legal review for match language and tax statements.

**Owner:** Marketing Ops Lead · **Approvers:** Creative Director, Development Director, Comms Lead
