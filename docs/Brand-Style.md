# Brand Style Guide — Giving Tuesday (Demo)

**Tagline:** *Clarity to impact—faster.*

## Voice & Tone
- Helpful, concise, confident without hype; plain US English (Grade 7–8).  
- Donor-centric (“you”-focused); active verbs; short sentences.

## Messaging Pillars
1. **Impact first** — show outcomes people enable.  
2. **Urgency, not pressure** — one day to act.  
3. **Trust** — transparency and stewardship.

## Writing Rules
- Spell out one–nine; numerals 10+. Oxford comma; AP dates (e.g., Dec. 2, 2025).  
- Avoid exclamation points; never ALL CAPS. Inclusive, people-first language.

## Accessibility
- Alt text (≤125 chars); AA contrast; meaningful links; visible focus rings.

## Email
- **Subject lines:** ≤55 chars; benefit first; ≤1 emoji optional.  
- **Preheaders:** complement subject; ≤85 chars.  
- **CTAs:** single clear verb — *Give now*, *Share the link*, *Pledge monthly*.  
- **Footers:** EIN/tax language + contact options.

## Social
- Captions ≤120 chars; square image or ≤15s video with subtitles.  
- Hashtags: `#GivingTuesday #GiveToDoGood` (2–3 total).

## Web
- Hero ≤7 words; subhead ≤16 words.  
- Primary button: **Give now**; Secondary: **Learn more**.  
- Progress bar updates at 25/50/75/100% milestones.

## Brand Elements
- **Primary:** Accessible Blue `#166EB4`  
- **Neutrals:** `#333333`, `#666666`, `#F5F6F8`  
- **Accent:** Emerald `#2BAE66`  
- **Type:** Segoe UI / system sans; headings semi-bold

## Examples
- **Subject lines:** “Today only: your gift goes twice as far”; “One day. One goal.”; “Make an impact before midnight.”  
- **Alt text:** “Volunteer hands a blanket to an elderly woman in a shelter.”
